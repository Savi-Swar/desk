## Recommended permanent filter

Adopt in `study_longshot.py`: join marks to labels, parse `closedTime` (formats: `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS[.ffffff]+00`, may be empty), and **drop a T-h mark when closedTime <= endDate - h hours**; keep marks with missing/unparseable closedTime (9 markets, 0.0%) but report the count. Optionally also cluster verdict cells by closedTime month rather than endDate month for early-resolvers.
